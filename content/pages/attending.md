<div class="parking-section">
<h2>Parking</h2>

<div class="venue-cards">
<div class="venue-card">
<h3>Recommended parking: State St. Lot A</h3>
<p>State St. Lot A is free for Data-Driven Wisconsin attendees until it fills. It is shared parking, but summer occupancy is light, so spots are easy to find. It is a one-block walk to Diercks Hall, past the construction site for MSOE's new engineering building.</p>
<p><a href="https://maps.apple/p/c_vKc~0C_-LRbB" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open in Apple Maps</a> &middot; <a href="https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&amp;query=MSOE+State+St.+Lot+A+Milwaukee+WI" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open in Google Maps</a></p>
</div>

<div class="venue-card">
<h3>Overflow: Water St. garage</h3>
<p>The Water St. parking garage is on the far side of the Campus Center. It is paid parking and an easy walk to Diercks Hall.</p>
<p><a href="https://maps.apple/p/q5MY8M9z6B7HC_" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open in Apple Maps</a> &middot; <a href="https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&amp;query=MSOE+Water+Street+parking+structure+Milwaukee+WI" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open in Google Maps</a></p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="warning-box alert-danger">
<p><strong>Do not park here:</strong> The MSOE lot at the corner of Milwaukee St. and State St. is for Grohmann Museum visitors only.</p>
</div>

<div class="warning-box">
<p><strong>Street parking:</strong> Nearby street parking is time-limited — 2 to 5 hours depending on the block, some metered, some free. Fine for a partial-day visit; risky for the full conference.</p>
</div>

<h3>Finding the buildings</h3>
<p>Diercks Hall, 1025 N. Milwaukee St. — registration, keynotes, Track 1 sessions, breakfast, lunch, and the sponsor expo.</p>
<p>Direct Supply ITC, 1020 N. Broadway — Track 2 sessions, the cocktail reception, and networking dinner, a short walk north of Diercks Hall.</p>

<details class="directions">
<summary>Driving directions</summary>
<p><strong>From the north:</strong> Take I-43 south to downtown Milwaukee. Take the Hwy 145 east / McKinley Ave. exit (73A). Turn left on McKinley and go six blocks to Broadway, then turn right toward campus and follow parking signs to State St. Lot A or the Water St. garage.</p>
<p><strong>From the south:</strong> Take I-94 west to downtown Milwaukee. Take the Hwy 145 east / McKinley Ave. exit (73A). Continue on McKinley six blocks to Broadway, turn right toward campus, and follow parking signs to State St. Lot A or the Water St. garage.</p>
<p><strong>From the west:</strong> Take I-94 east to downtown Milwaukee, then I-43 north toward Green Bay (exit 310B). Take the Hwy 145 east / McKinley Ave. exit (73A). Continue on McKinley six blocks to Broadway, turn right toward campus, and follow parking signs to State St. Lot A or the Water St. garage.</p>
</details>
