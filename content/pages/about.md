The Data-Driven Wisconsin (DDWI) conference was born in 2016 as a long-form event out of the Milwuakee and Madison big data meetup scene.
Our goal is to offer a high-quality event at a reasonable price that brings together Wisconsin-based data and AI practicioners
for learning and networking.
Great local speakers sharing their lessons learned from putting the latest data and AI technologies into practice.
Over 200 industry professionals, predominently in the software, data, and AI fields, attend each year.
We paused for a few years after the COVID pandemic, but we returned in 2024 and has been running continuously since.
